Inland & Hot

Calallen's Heat Has Nowhere to Hide

Calallen sits on the northwest edge of Corpus Christi along US-77, far enough back from the water that the bay breeze mostly gives out before it gets here. That's a mixed bag for your AC. The good news is there's a lot less salt in the air, so outdoor units don't corrode the way they do out on the coast. The trade-off is the heat — inland Calallen runs a few degrees hotter and drier than the waterfront, and there's no afternoon sea breeze to take the edge off.

That means a Calallen system runs long and hard from spring well into fall, and a unit that's a little undersized or overdue for service starts falling behind on the worst afternoons. With all the newer subdivisions and growing families out here, plenty of homes are leaning on builder-grade systems that need a closer eye than folks expect. We know what this dry inland heat asks of an air conditioner, and how to keep one ahead of it.
