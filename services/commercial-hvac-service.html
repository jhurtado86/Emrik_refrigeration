What We Do

Full-Service Commercial HVAC for the Coastal Bend

From a single emergency repair to a maintenance contract for your whole property, we keep commercial systems running. Here's what we handle for businesses across Corpus Christi and the surrounding coast.

Commercial AC Repair & Service

When a commercial system goes down, fast and accurate is what matters. We service and repair rooftop package units, commercial split systems, and mini-split and VRF setups, along with the controls that run them. Our techs find the real cause — not just the symptom — across compressors, condenser and evaporator fans, contactors and capacitors, refrigerant charge, economizers, and thermostats or building controls.

South Texas heat keeps commercial equipment under heavy load most of the year, and on the coast, salt air and humidity corrode rooftop units faster than inland. We know what to look for, carry the common commercial parts, and work to get you back up the same day. Because we answer 24/7, an after-hours failure doesn't have to mean a closed business the next morning.

Commercial AC Installation & Replacement

When a unit is past saving, an aging system is draining your power bill, or you're building out a new space, we install and replace commercial HVAC sized for the job. We start with a proper load calculation for your building — square footage, occupancy, kitchen heat, big windows, and the long South Texas cooling season — so you don't end up undersized and struggling or oversized and short-cycling.

We handle rooftop units, split systems, and multi-zone installs, coordinate crane and curb work when a rooftop changeout calls for it, and lay out options at different efficiency levels with the payback explained in plain numbers. Free commercial estimates mean you know the cost up front, and we install clean, to code, with the least disruption to your operation we can manage.

Commercial HVAC Maintenance Contracts

The businesses that avoid emergency breakdowns are the ones on a maintenance plan. A commercial maintenance contract with Emrik is a scheduled relationship, not a one-time visit — we come out on a regular cycle to clean coils, check refrigerant and electrical, change filters, and inspect belts and economizers, catching small problems before they shut you down during peak hours.

For coastal buildings that regular attention matters even more: salt air and humidity wear rooftop units hard, and a unit that's cleaned and tuned runs more efficiently, uses less power, and lasts years longer. Contract customers also get priority response when something does go wrong. We build the plan around your equipment and your budget — straight terms, no hidden costs, and a partner who already knows your building.

Restaurant & Kitchen HVAC Service

Restaurants are about the toughest HVAC environment there is. A hot kitchen, exhaust hoods pulling conditioned air out, makeup air units, rooftop units running hard, and a dining room that has to stay comfortable while the line stays survivable. Get the balance wrong and you've got an uncomfortable dining room, an unbearable kitchen, or negative pressure tugging the front doors shut.

We service the whole picture for restaurants and commercial kitchens across Corpus Christi — rooftop units, makeup air, exhaust fan motors, and the balance between them — plus the comfort cooling out front. Keeping a kitchen at safe temperatures isn't only about comfort; it's a health-code matter, and we treat it that way. We answer 24/7, because a restaurant can't run a dinner service with the HVAC down.
